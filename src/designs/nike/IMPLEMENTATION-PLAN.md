# Nike-inspired Eleven Forward concept

Campaign-led white storefront with oversized centered Inter type, quiet gray product wells, compact pill actions, and black/white editorial panels. Keep Eleven Forward imagery, identity and existing mission copy. No competitor marks or language. Home pairs a wide P001 hero with a real three-product edit, Survival Club campaign and founder story. Collection is a clean three-column merchandise grid. Product pages have generous image galleries and a sticky shared purchase panel. Survival Club combines a typographic campaign with explicit product cards and garment details. All routes use ctx.url; controls and commerce remain shared. CSS is scoped to data-design=nike. Mobile stacks editorial panels and uses two-column collection cards; motion is optional and focus remains visible.
