# Survival Club image generation prompts

Built-in image generation was used. The retail tee reference controls garment cut and visible fabric appearance; original Eleven Forward mockups control artwork, colors and placement. Exact textile composition is not established by the screenshot. All generated views are concepts.

## black-front

Use case: product-mockup. Create one photoreal catalog image of a single black Eleven Forward tee, FRONT view square to camera. Input 1 is the blank garment CUT AND FABRIC reference: reproduce the premium retail tee's relaxed straight body, set-in shoulders, gently dropped shoulder line, moderate sleeve length to mid upper arm, narrow ribbed crew neck, smooth fine matte jersey with soft light folds, straight hem and natural drape. Input 2 is ARTWORK ONLY reference, NOT shirt silhouette: preserve the exact print design and placement on the FRONT: small wearer-left chest ELEVEN FORWARD over SURVIVAL CLUB, same typography and relative placement; NO large back graphic on front. Black fabric with original Volt yellow-green print. Render garment alone on an invisible support with no model, no mannequin or hanger visible. Square composition, entire garment in frame with 8% margin, pale gray neutral studio background and soft consistent lighting. No other items, no supplier webpage content, no borders, no labels, no additional text. The reference model is only to show the garment shape; do not reproduce the person. Match visible construction and print faithfully.

## Transparent background extraction (all six tees, cap angle and cap pair)

Use case: background-extraction. Remove ONLY the studio background and floor shadow from this exact product image, replacing them with genuine alpha transparency. Preserve every garment/cap pixel and all print lettering, logos, distressed graphics, fabric texture, colors, seams and silhouette. Do not regenerate, redraw, smooth, recolor or reposition the product. Preserve original image dimensions, framing, product scale and margins. Keep both products when there are two caps. No added shadows, glow, background, checkerboard artwork or other changes.

## White front/back alpha cleanup

Precise alpha-matte cleanup only. Remove isolated opaque white specks and ragged halo pixels outside the true garment silhouette. Preserve the tee interior, graphics, seams, folds, colors, frame and positioning. Use a natural antialiased garment contour and genuine transparency without detached fragments.
